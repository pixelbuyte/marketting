# Skintel — Founding Offer Conversion Kit

**Goal:** get people to pay $20 for the founding batch *before* the iOS app is on the App Store.
**The one idea that makes this work:** it's not a pre-order. Pro is live on the web today. The $20 buys something that works the moment you pay; the apps are a free add-on at launch. Every asset below leads with that, because "lock in before iOS" alone is a promise, and strangers don't pay for promises.

**Facts you can state (all true on the site as of Sep 14, 2026):**
- $20, one payment, 3 months of Pro, lists at $49.99, code applied automatically
- No subscription, no auto-renew, no card kept on file
- 14-day refund, no questions
- 500 founding seats, live counter on the page
- Founding Member number on your account, first invite to the iOS beta
- Web Pro works today; iOS and Android included when they ship

**Do not say:** "limited time" (it's seat-limited, not time-limited), "clears acne," "dermatologist," any result claim. The tool finds a correlation; it doesn't promise a cure.

**Where to send people:** `skinstel.com/discount` — the full pitch with FAQ and guarantee. Say the domain letter by letter in captions: **s-k-i-n-s-t-e-l**. People who hear "Skintel" will type the wrong site.

## 7. What to measure (per asset)

- Views → **clicks to /discount** (the metric that matters; views alone are vanity)
- /discount pageviews → `founding_claim_click` events (already tracked in Vercel Analytics)
- `founding_claim_click` → completed Stripe checkouts
- Refund rate inside 14 days

A gap between pageviews and claim clicks is a copy problem. A gap after the claim click is a checkout problem. The site already instruments both.
